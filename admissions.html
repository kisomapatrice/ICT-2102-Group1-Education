Admissions FAQs

Questions families often ask.

Is Bright Future a day or boarding school?

Bright Future Secondary School is listed as a mixed day and boarding school. Contact the school to confirm current availability and the arrangements for each option.

How can I get the current fees structure?

Contact the school directly and request the latest official fees structure, including any day or boarding charges and payment guidance.

Which classes can I apply for?

Make an enquiry with the learner's current class and previous school details. The school can guide you on available places and appropriate class placement.

Can I visit the school before applying?

Yes. Contact the school to arrange a convenient visit and confirm who you should meet when you arrive.

Which documents should I prepare?

Ask the admissions office for the current checklist. It may include previous school records and learner identification details, depending on the class and entry requirements.
