PREPARE WITH CONFIDENCE

Four steps to feel more organised.

Admission rules vary between institutions. Use this checklist to prepare, then confirm all requirements with the official admissions office.

01

Explore your options

Review the learning area, entry requirements, duration, and learning format.

02

Confirm requirements

Check the official application dates, required documents, and submission method.

03

Prepare documents

Gather the relevant academic results and identification documents requested by the institution.

04

Ask before paying

Confirm approved payment channels, fee amounts, and receipts with an authorised office.
