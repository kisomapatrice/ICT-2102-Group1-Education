SKILLS THAT TRAVEL WITH YOU

Build a strong foundation.

Whatever your learning area, effective study involves more than remembering facts.

Digital confidence

  • Use files, browsers, and learning tools
  • Check source credibility
  • Protect your online accounts

Learning habits

  • Plan study time and set goals
  • Take notes and review key ideas
  • Ask useful questions and reflect

Communication

  • Explain ideas clearly
  • Work respectfully in teams
  • Present evidence and conclusions
