BRIGHT FUTURE SECONDARY SCHOOL — PHOTO SOURCES
===============================================

The website currently displays real Bright Future Secondary School, Kaliro photographs
from the WordPress Campus Connect coverage of the school visit on 16 July 2026.

Original photo story:
https://events.wordpress.org/campusconnect/2026/central-eastern-ug/wordpress-campus-connect-lights-up-kaliro-discovering-talent-at-bright-future-secondary-school/

The photos are currently loaded from their original WordPress-hosted image URLs. This
keeps the package smaller, but the website needs an internet connection to display them.
Before official handover/public launch, obtain permission from the school and the
photographer/publisher to republish identifiable learners, especially minors, on the
school website. If approved, download approved originals into this folder and update
the image URLs in the HTML to local assets/images/ filenames.
