Learning should feel within reach.

Group 1 Education brings learning information, study tools, and trusted educational resources together in one clear, accessible place.

OUR PURPOSE

A useful digital space,
built by learners.

The learning hub brings together study topics, digital confidence, academic organisation, and shared learning. Each section is arranged to make useful information easier to find and revisit.

01

Clarity

Present information in sections that are easy to scan, understand, and revisit.

02

Access

Keep essential controls reachable and layouts usable across different screen sizes.

03

Practice

Use working interactions to turn classroom concepts into practical web skills.

04

Teamwork

Share tasks, review one another's work, and test changes before publishing.

HOW WE WORK

Four roles.
One shared result.

The four roles share responsibility for structure, content, testing, and presentation. Review one another’s work and keep decisions visible to the whole group.

H

HTML Developer

Builds semantic page structure, navigation, tables, and forms.

C

Content Designer

Checks the clarity, accuracy, order, and consistency of the information.

T

Tester

Checks links, validation, keyboard access, responsive views, and errors.

P

Presenter / Team Leader

Coordinates progress, explains the final work, and confirms that group tasks are shared.
