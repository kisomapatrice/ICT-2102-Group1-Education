Who We Are

Kaliro Bright Future College is presented as a learning community where students are encouraged to study, create, communicate and serve their communities.

We value discipline, teamwork and continuous learning while giving learners opportunities to build useful academic and digital skills.

Our Learning Values

  • ✓Respect for teachers, fellow learners and the wider community.
  • ✓Responsible use of technology and learning resources.
  • ✓Teamwork, creativity and problem-solving.
  • ✓A commitment to lifelong learning and personal improvement.
